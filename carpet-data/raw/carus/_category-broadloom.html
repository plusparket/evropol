Original: Natural Sea Grass Flooring for a Timeless, Primitive Touch

Introducing Original, a flooring solution that brings a beautifully primitive feel to any space with a simple, yet striking color. Made from 100% sea grass, this natural flooring option is denser than its counterpart, Chevron, offering a robust, lasting design. With a total weight of 2250 grams, Original provides a durable, eco-friendly surface perfect for homes that value both style and sustainability.
Available in a convenient 4-meter width, Original is designed to fit a variety of spaces throughout the home. Certified for medium residential use, it’s ideal for living rooms, bedrooms, and hallways, where it will add a warm, earthy vibe. The latex foam backing enhances comfort and ease of installation, making it a versatile choice for many interior styles. Bring nature into your home with Original—the perfect blend of beauty, strength, and natural elegance.

Chevron: Artisan Sea Grass Flooring with Natural Elegance

Experience the beauty of nature with Chevron, a handcrafted flooring solution made from 100% sea grass. This unique flooring features smooth, soft fibers derived from an aquatic plant, offering both durability and a beautifully natural appearance. Weighing 2700 g/m², Chevron is a robust choice that will elevate any space with its timeless, earthy color—perfect for creating a warm, inviting atmosphere in your home.
The latex foam backing enhances comfort and ease of installation, making Chevron a practical addition to all types of living spaces. With its exceptional resistance and non-absorptive properties, it stands as a true force of nature, combining strength with aesthetic appeal. Ideal for those looking for a sustainable yet stylish flooring option, Chevron is the perfect choice for any room where natural beauty and lasting performance are a priority.

Nova : Comfort and Performance for the Hospitality Industry

 

The Nova collection is a high-end saxony carpet, specially designed to meet the demands of the hospitality sector. Made from 100% polyamide, it ensures durability and easy maintenance, even in high-traffic areas. Available in 14 solid colors, Nova allows you to create elegant and welcoming atmospheres, ideal for guest rooms and common areas. With a pile weight of 900 g/m², a usage rating of Class 32, and a fire rating of Bfl‑s1, the Nova collection combines comfort, safety, and long-lasting performance for hotel environments.

BRUT: Refined Design and Warm Ambiance

 

The BRUT collection combines a graphic design inspired by cracked concrete with a super-soft, refined surface, creating a modern yet warm atmosphere, perfect for the hospitality sector. Made from 100% polyamide, piece-dyed, with a pile weight of 510 g (52 pts), this saxony carpet provides comfort and durability for medium-traffic areas. Available in 6 colors, BRUT blends style and functionality, with a Class 31 rating and Cfl-s1 fire classification, ensuring welcoming and safe spaces.
